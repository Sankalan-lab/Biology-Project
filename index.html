THE BASICS

What is Genetics?

Genetics is the branch of biology that studies genes, heredity and variation in living organisms.

DNA

DNA

DNA stores the genetic instructions used for growth, development and functioning of organisms.

GENE

Genes

A gene is a functional section of DNA that contains information for a specific biological function or product.

CHR

Chromosomes

Chromosomes are structures made mainly of DNA and proteins that organize genetic material.

MOLECULAR GENETICS

DNA & Chromosomes

DNA contains the biological information that helps organisms grow, develop and reproduce.

Double Helix

DNA consists of two complementary strands arranged in a double-helix structure.

Four Bases

The four main nitrogenous bases are adenine, thymine, guanine and cytosine.

Base Pairing

Adenine pairs with thymine, while guanine pairs with cytosine.

Genetic Information

The sequence of bases stores biological information used by cells.

GENETIC CONDITIONS

Genetic Disorders

Genetic disorders can result from changes in genes, chromosomes or other genetic material.

Chromosomal

Down Syndrome

Usually caused by an extra copy of chromosome 21.

Single-gene

Sickle Cell Disease

A genetic condition involving changes in the beta-globin gene.

X-linked

Haemophilia

A group of inherited bleeding disorders involving clotting factor deficiencies.

Genetic

Thalassemia

An inherited blood disorder affecting haemoglobin production.

Autosomal dominant

Huntington's Disease

An inherited disorder caused by a change in the HTT gene.

Chromosomal

Turner Syndrome

A chromosomal condition involving complete or partial absence of one X chromosome.

Genetic Inheritance

Inheritance describes how genetic information is passed from parents to offspring.

Autosomal Dominant

A single altered copy of a gene can be sufficient for the trait or condition to occur.

Autosomal Recessive

A condition generally occurs when an individual inherits two altered copies of the relevant gene.

X-linked

The relevant gene is located on the X chromosome.

Mitochondrial

Mitochondrial DNA is generally inherited through the maternal line.

Types of Mutations

01
